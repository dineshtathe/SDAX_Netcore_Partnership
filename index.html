How Netcore can support the journey

Investor signals.
Coordinated next steps.

Illustrative workflows to evaluate, not descriptions of live SDAX campaigns. The proposed Netcore role is to run agreed journeys using authorised data and approved rules. Content about investment products would remain subject to SDAX’s own compliance review.

Journey 01

Help new investors finish account setup.

A signal-led follow-up for a selected step in account opening, replacing the chosen follow-up process rather than adding a parallel one.

Illustrative use case
Receive

Setup step not completed

Take an authorised event from the sign-up flow (website or app, to be confirmed), match it to a permissioned user and check eligibility for follow-up.

Execute

Step-specific next action

After an agreed wait, recheck status and contact rules, then sequence approved content on eligible channels, pointing to the exact step still open.

Adapt

Exit on completion

Stop pending steps when the matched completion event arrives. Validate messages already in flight.

Proposed execution ownership: Netcore would run event entry, audience checks, channel delivery, contact limits, completion exits and reporting. Identity verification, eligibility decisions and account status stay in SDAX systems.

Measures to agreeSetup completion rate for the selected step · time to completion · repeat-contact rate · workflow effort · event-to-action time

Journey 02

Guide funded accounts towards a first investment decision.

Coordinate educational and informational communication for users who have an account but have not yet transacted, within SDAX’s own content and compliance rules.

Illustrative use case
Receive

Account ready, no first investment yet

Use approved eligibility rules, investor-category rules and consent status to define who may receive which content.

Execute

Approved, informational content

Sequence SDAX-approved material (how the platform works, how listings are structured) across eligible channels, avoiding overlapping prompts.

Adapt

Recognise first investment

When the matched first-transaction event arrives, stop onboarding prompts and move to the agreed next step.

Proposed execution ownership: Netcore would own sequencing, delivery and suppression for the selected journey. SDAX retains product information, offer eligibility, content approval and any investment-related disclosures.

Measures to agreeFirst-investment rate vs a control group · time to first investment · messages per user · opt-outs · journey-change effort

Journey 03

Turn website interest into a coordinated next step.

Connect an identified, consented website or event-registration action to the same journey logic, so interest expressed on the site is followed up once, in the right channel, and stops when the user acts.

Illustrative use case
Receive

Interest signal on the site

Candidate signals include a content or event registration, or an incomplete sign-up. Which signals can be captured, identified and consented must be confirmed.

Execute

One coordinated follow-up

Apply investor-category rules, frequency caps and approved content, instead of separate follow-ups from different tools or teams.

Adapt

Stop on action or opt-out

Suppress further messages when a matched sign-up, investment or opt-out event is received.

Proposed execution ownership: Netcore would own the agreed audience, sequence, delivery and reporting. SDAX keeps the website, lead and customer records, and decides who may be offered which product and what may be said about it.

Measures to agreeRegistration-to-sign-up rate vs control · opt-outs · messages after action · operating effort · total cost

Boundaries & validation

Clear ownership.
Evidence before expansion.

Permissions & contact

Confirm identity matching, consent, eligible channels and suppression rules for each journey and channel owner, including differences between investor categories and between website visitors and account holders.

Integration & events

Identify source events, latency and ownership across the website, app, CRM and chat. Test duplicate handling, retries and exits before relying on them.

Regulatory & data controls

SDAX’s compliance function would need to review communications about investment products, and validate hosting, data residency, access, retention and security against its requirements and applicable MAS expectations. Netcore publishes compliance statements; they need review for this use. [7]

Measurement & migration

Agree a baseline, a control group and complete costs. Plan cutover so old and new journeys do not run in parallel, and decide which messages stay outside scope.
